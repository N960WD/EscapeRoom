How to Play

  1. Before the first card, agree on your limits and a stop word.
  2. Take turns tapping a numbered card. Each card can only be played once.
  3. The deck plays in three rounds: Warm-Up (1–14), Heat (15–23) and Main Event (24–30). Cards are shuffled within each round, and the next round unlocks when the current one is finished.
  4. The card flips and splits into two halves, each with a short clue. A is always the gentler pick, B the bolder one.
  5. Choose one clue — it flips to reveal the activity. “You” means the player who picked the card.
  6. Pass — each player has a set number of passes (chosen at the start). Tap Pass as soon as the card flips, or after you've revealed the activity, and you pick a new card right away. Out of passes? You do the activity. Tap a player's name to see how many passes they have left.
  7. Stop — either player can say “stop” at any time. The activity ends and the next player takes their turn.
  8. The game ends when every card has been played. No points, no winner.

Supply list: scarf or soft tie, blindfold, toy, ice, a drink, massage oil, massage candle, lube, towels, a playlist, and a mirror.

Works fully offline. Your game is saved on this device automatically.
