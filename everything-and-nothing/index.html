How to Play

  1. Two players take turns tapping a numbered card (1–30). Each card can only be played once.
  2. The card flips and splits into two halves, each with a short clue.
  3. Choose one clue — it flips to reveal the activity.
  4. Activities may be something you do, something you do for or to the other player, or something they do for you.
  5. Pass — each player has 3. Passing lets you pick a new card right away. Out of passes? You must do the activity.
  6. Stop — either player can say “stop” at any time. The activity ends and the next player takes their turn.
  7. The game ends when all 30 cards have been played. No points, no winner — just fun.

Works fully offline. Your game is saved on this device automatically.

Edit Cards
